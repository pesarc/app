# Description of Business Activity — Pesarc

Ready-to-paste descriptions for CAC objects and license/registration forms.
Framed as a technology + payments platform, non-custodial, working through
licensed partners — accurate, and it avoids over-claiming regulated activities
you don't yet hold a licence for. Not legal advice; have counsel confirm the
exact wording for any regulated filing.

## One line (form field, e.g. CAC "nature of business")
> The development and operation of a financial-technology platform that enables
> individuals and businesses to send, receive, hold and manage money across
> borders in their local currencies through digital, mobile, USSD and agent
> channels.

## Short paragraph (license / registration "description of business activity")
> Pesarc is a financial-technology company that builds and operates software for
> fast, low-cost cross-border payments and money transfers. Through a mobile app,
> a conversational agent, USSD and web interfaces, the platform lets individuals
> and businesses send, receive, hold and manage funds in their own local
> currency, with settlement handled by technology and cash-in and cash-out
> processed through licensed banking and mobile-money partners. Pesarc operates on
> a non-custodial basis: users retain control of their own funds, and the company
> does not hold customer deposits. The business also provides payment tools and an
> API that allow merchants and other businesses to accept and reconcile payments.

## Objects clause (CAC memorandum — numbered)
1. To design, develop, operate and maintain financial-technology software and
   digital platforms for payments, money transfer, and the sending, receiving,
   holding and management of funds across borders in local currencies.
2. To provide payment-processing tools, application programming interfaces (APIs)
   and hosted checkout services enabling merchants and businesses to accept,
   settle and reconcile payments.
3. To facilitate cash-in and cash-out to and from bank accounts and mobile-money
   wallets through partnerships with duly licensed financial institutions and
   payment service providers.
4. To develop and deploy software, mobile applications, USSD services and
   agent-assisted and messaging-based interfaces in connection with the foregoing.
5. To carry on the business of software development, information technology and
   related consultancy and services.

## Notes on positioning
- **Say "technology / software / platform" and "through licensed partners."**
  It's accurate and keeps you on the software-and-partner side of the line rather
  than presenting as an unlicensed deposit-taking or money-transmission operator.
- **Emphasise non-custodial** ("users retain control of their own funds; the
  company does not hold customer deposits"). This materially changes how a
  regulator reads the activity.
- **Avoid "cryptocurrency exchange", "trading digital assets", "deposits",**
  unless and until you hold the matching licence — that language invites the
  heavier CBN/SEC regime. Stablecoin settlement is an implementation detail, not
  the described activity.
