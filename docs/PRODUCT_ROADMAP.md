# Pesarc product roadmap (founder direction, 2026-09)

Ongoing priority: **UX must keep improving**, with retailers as the primary
audience (import/export retailers in the Global South, SMEs, businesses, and
SaaS/API consumers). Never mention the "no dollar/USDC in the path" angle in any
marketing copy. Never use the em dash (—).

## Feature backlog
- **USSD payment support** (feature phones, no smartphone/data needed).
- **Developer API** for SMEs and SaaS to integrate payments. An OPay-style in-app
  pay UX: partners can send users into Pesarc to pay, then redirect back.
- **WhatsApp agent** alongside the in-app Pesarc AI agent, so users pay
  interactively in chat. Both the WhatsApp agent and the Pesarc agent must
  support **voice input**.
- **Bills**: Airtime / Data / Electricity, paid via the agents. UX must be
  beautiful.
- **On-chain African stocks** + **prediction-market creation** (users create
  markets).
- **Business vs retail split**: the business section is a different experience and
  must NOT be shown to retail users. Route users to the right surface during
  onboarding (pick retail vs business), then send them to the appropriate app.
